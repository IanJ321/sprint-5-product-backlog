# Sprint 5 — Proposed User Stories

Status: Proposed for team review
Total proposed story points: 24

Priorities and estimates are provisional and will be discussed during
Sprint Planning.

## 1. Coordinate backlog grooming across teams

As the Product Owner, I want to coordinate backlog grooming with the other product team so that both teams work from aligned priorities, avoid duplicate work, and understand cross-team dependencies.

- Priority: 5
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 1

## 2. Update and test the credits page

As a visitor, I want the credits page to include everyone so that I can identify the people contributing to the product.

- Priority: 3
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 1

## 3. Implement and verify CI/CD

As a developer, I want to implement and verify a CI/CD workflow for development, test, and production so that my changes can be checked and deployed consistently across all three environments.

- Priority: 5
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 5

## 4. Use the product and contribute improvement stories

As a team member, I want to use the product from a user’s perspective and contribute at least two valuable improvement stories so that future development addresses observed user needs.

- Priority: 4
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 2

## 5. Prepare for and participate in the Product Review

As a team member, I want to prepare for and participate in the Fall 2026 mid-term Product Review on Tuesday, October 27, at 2:00 p.m. CT so that reviewers can evaluate our progress and provide feedback on future priorities.

- Priority: 5
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 2

## 6. Confirm before deleting study materials

As a user, I want to confirm before permanently deleting a quiz or flashcard deck so that I do not accidentally lose my study materials.

- Priority: 4
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 3

## 7. Provide guidance when no study materials exist

As a new user, I want clear guidance when I have no quizzes or flashcard decks so that I know how to create my first study material.

- Priority: 2
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 3

## 8. Warn before leaving unsaved quiz changes

As a user creating or editing a quiz, I want a warning before leaving with unsaved changes so that I do not accidentally lose my work.

- Priority: 4
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 5

## 9. Display quiz progress

As a user taking a quiz, I want to see my current question number and the total number of questions so that I can understand how far I have progressed.

- Priority: 3
- Type: Story
- Proposed story points: 2
